One word for an ex, entered in the [Describe Your Ex in One Word](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-describe-your-ex-in-one-word-9fc6ebe7) bounty on make.fun.

## The word

**Understudy**

An understudy learns every line, waits in the wings, and only ever plays the
lead if the star can't make it. My ex was never the lead in my life — just the
stand-in who rehearsed the role and never got the part. Dry, exact, and a
whole breakup story in a single word.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else, no network,
no randomness, fully deterministic.

```
~/node22/bin/node main.ts
```
